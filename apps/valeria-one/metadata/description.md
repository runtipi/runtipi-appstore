# Valeria One

The open-source back office for any business — self-hosted, in one app.

Valeria One gives a small business its whole front desk:

- A website with an **online store** (cart & checkout) and **online bookings**
- A **CRM** with follow-ups and **NPS** surveys
- **Invoicing**, including Mexican **CFDI 4.0**
- **Gift cards**
- An **AI website assistant** that streams its answers and can speak and listen
- **WhatsApp, SMS, missed-call text-back and voicemail**

AI, messaging and payments are optional and use **your own** provider accounts (OpenAI/Anthropic/Gemini, Twilio or Meta, Stripe, Facturapi) — so there's no per-use cost and nothing phones home. It runs fine with no AI key at all.

After the app starts, create your admin account from the **Server** page or a setup link — see the [project README](https://github.com/valeriaone/valeria-one). Licensed AGPL-3.0.
